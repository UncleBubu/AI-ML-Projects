RAIN HANDLES TRAINING AND CERTIFICATIONS
 RAIN is a research and training facility dedicated to robotics and artificial intelligence technologies. RAIN operates on a standard that is unparalleled in the fast-evolving Robotics and Artificial Intelligence industry and operates out of its research and training facility located in Ibadan, Nigeria. With an average of 30 trainees at every training cohort program, and with a host of highly skilled developers and academics, RAIN presents high competency on a global scale and across different standards. 



Till date, RAIN has welcomed different foreign diplomats to its facility in Nigeria, such as the Deputy Secretary General of the United Nations, the Canadian Trade Commissioner to Nigeria, a delegation from the United States Consulate and from the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) Nigerian office. RAIN has also been a technical partner for the AI4Dev competition organized by Nile University, Egypt 2020 and a facilitator to the MIT hosted entrepreneurship boot camp tagged LaunchX 2022. These are all attestations to the high standard and quality RAIN has represented through the years. 



Graduates from RAIN are all over the world, contributing outstandingly to the growth of AI and Robotics. Some have used their RAIN certification to cross into masters and PhD programs in European and American Universities.

MEET OUR FOUNDER, DR OLUSOLA AYOOLA
 

Dr. Olusola Ayoola is the Founder and CEO of Robotics and Artificial Intelligence Nigeria (RAIN), a leading organisation in artificial intelligence and robotics talent and product development across Africa. With a strong academic background in AI, robotics, and engineering, Dr. Ayoola is a visionary leader committed to leveraging cutting-edge technologies to solve critical challenges in sectors like healthcare, national security, education, and sustainability.



He has spearheaded multiple groundbreaking projects, including AI-based solutions such as a Passenger Scream Detection System for Public Buses, Exoskeletons for Upper Limb Dysfunction Rehabilitation, and Carbon Emission Tracker Drones for environmental surveillance. 



His work extends to smart devices aimed at improving healthcare outcomes, like the Smart Arm Gyroscopic System for patients with Erb’s palsy and a Smart Drug Dispenser for dementia patients.

Under his leadership, RAIN is also expanding its training programs to 20 cities across Africa, fostering the next generation of AI and robotics experts. Dr. Ayoola's passion for education and knowledge-sharing is evident through his numerous keynote speeches and workshops, including at the prestigious ICARS 2023 Conference in Abu Dhabi, UAE, and his involvement with the Nigerian Society of Engineers, where he has inspired young engineers to explore career opportunities in AI and the Internet of Things (IoT).



Dr. Ayoola’s contributions extend beyond RAIN's projects; he is an advocate for developing sustainable technologies to address Africa’s unique challenges. His leadership has earned him recognition as a thought leader in the African tech ecosystem, making him a sought-after speaker and collaborator in global technology and innovation forums.
